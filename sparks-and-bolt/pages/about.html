About Sparks & Bolt Electrical

Our Story

Sparks & Bolt Electrical Services is a small, family-run electrical contracting business based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Founded in 2015 by a qualified electrician, we began by servicing residential clients in the Johannesburg CBD and Braamfontein area before growing to include small commercial clients. Our reputation has been built entirely through word-of-mouth referrals and years of reliable, honest work.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable, and affordable electrical installation, repair, and compliance services to homes and small businesses across Johannesburg.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted electrical service provider in the greater Johannesburg area, known for punctuality, safety compliance, and honest pricing.

Why Choose Us

  • Fully qualified and insured electricians
  • Certificate of Compliance (COC) issued for all applicable work
  • Transparent, upfront pricing with no hidden costs
  • Emergency callouts available for urgent faults
  • Over a decade of trusted service in the Johannesburg community
